## Selected Products

### 01 · [Widhura IT](https://widhura.lk)

The central Widhura platform for software systems, business solutions, computer services, digital presence, and radio/media technology. Widhura supports local businesses from its Udugama and Makandura hubs while delivering digital products beyond Sri Lanka.

`Software Systems` `Business Solutions` `Digital Presence` `Radio & Media`

### 02 · [Kadapela LK](https://kadapela.lk)

A Sri Lankan online marketplace designed for convenient island-wide shopping. Features include product discovery, customer accounts, secure payments, delivery workflows, order tracking, product comparison, wishlists, warranty lookup, and affiliate commerce.

`E-commerce` `Marketplace Operations` `Payments` `Order Management`

### 03 · [JobXP](https://jobxp.lk)

A complete recruitment ecosystem connecting job seekers, companies, candidates, and opportunities. JobXP supports job discovery, professional CV creation, instant applications, employer job posting, company pages, candidate profiles, and location-based browsing.

`Recruitment Technology` `CV Builder` `Employer Tools` `Job Applications`

### 04 · [Hadawatha.lk](https://hadawatha.lk)

A multi-vendor marketplace connecting buyers and sellers through online stores, wholesale listings, consumer products, secure checkout, delivery and return support, and affiliate commerce.

`Multi-vendor Commerce` `Wholesale` `Seller Stores` `Affiliate Commerce`

### 05 · [Widhura POS](https://pos.widhura.lk)

A practical point-of-sale and operations system for ambitious shops, covering sales, inventory, barcode workflows, reports, and staff operations.

### 06 · [Widhura Dictionary](https://dictionary.widhura.lk)

A multilingual Windows dictionary and language-input system built around translation, phonetic typing, text-to-speech, and a large cross-language word database.

## Recognition & Product History

### Widhura Dictionary 2015 with Widhura Input

A language-technology product featured by CNET Download.com. The product provided a fast automated dictionary search experience across **14 languages** and approximately **259,000 words**, with cross-language translation and text-to-speech capabilities.

The accompanying **Widhura Input** tool enabled real-time Sinhala, Tamil, and English phonetic typing anywhere in Windows using an SMS-style input method.
